# Premium Payments & Auto-Debit Guide
Suraksha Life Insurance — Customer Help Centre, document PAY-01 (v3.2)

## 1. Payment modes
### 1.1 Accepted payment methods
Premiums can be paid by UPI, net banking, debit card, credit card, NACH auto-debit, or cheque/DD at any branch. Cash payments are accepted at branches only up to ₹49,999 per policy per day, and require a PAN card copy for amounts above ₹20,000.

### 1.2 Premium frequencies
Policies can be on annual, half-yearly, quarterly or monthly frequency. Monthly frequency is only available when the premium is paid through NACH auto-debit or a standing instruction on a card. Changing frequency takes effect from the next policy anniversary.

## 2. NACH auto-debit
### 2.1 What NACH is
NACH (National Automated Clearing House) is an RBI/NPCI system that lets Suraksha Life debit the premium directly from your bank account on the due date, so you never miss a payment.

### 2.2 Registering a NACH mandate (setting up auto-debit)
To set up auto-debit, fill the NACH Mandate form with your bank account number, IFSC code, bank name, the maximum debit amount (in figures and words) and frequency ("As & when presented" is recommended). Sign exactly as per your bank records. Registration takes 7–10 working days; until the mandate is active, pay the premium manually. E-mandate via net banking or debit card is also available in the app and activates within 48 hours.

### 2.3 Common reasons a mandate is rejected
The most common rejection reasons are: signature mismatch with bank records, wrong IFSC code, account type not eligible (e.g. PPF or loan accounts), amount in words not matching amount in figures, and overwriting on the form without a countersignature. A rejected mandate can be resubmitted free of charge.

### 2.4 Cancelling or changing a mandate
Submit a cancellation request at least 15 days before the next due date via the app, email, or branch. To change the bank account, register a new mandate first, then cancel the old one, so there is no gap in cover.

## 3. Missed payments
### 3.1 Grace period
If a premium is missed, you get a grace period of 30 days (15 days for monthly mode). Cover continues during the grace period. If a claim arises during the grace period, the unpaid premium is deducted from the claim amount.

### 3.2 Lapse and revival
If the premium is not paid by the end of the grace period, the policy lapses. A lapsed policy can be revived within 5 years from the first unpaid premium by paying all arrears with interest (currently 9.5% p.a.). Revival after 6 months requires a good-health declaration, and after 2 years may need a medical examination.
