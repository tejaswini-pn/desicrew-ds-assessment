# Claims Guide
Suraksha Life Insurance — Customer Help Centre, document CLM-03 (v4.0)

## 1. Death claims
### 1.1 How to intimate a claim
The nominee can intimate a claim via the app, the 24x7 helpline 1800-000-1234, email to claims@surakshalife.example, or any branch. Intimation should be made as early as possible; there is no strict deadline, but delays may require an explanation letter.

### 1.2 Documents required
Claim form, original or attested death certificate, policy document, nominee's photo ID and address proof, cancelled cheque or bank statement of the nominee, and — for deaths within 3 years of policy start — medical records and the hospital discharge summary. For accidental deaths, the FIR and post-mortem report are also needed.

### 1.3 Settlement timeline
IRDAI regulations require settlement within 15 days of receiving all documents. If investigation is required (typically for claims within the first 3 years), it must be completed within 45 days. Suraksha Life's 2025 claim settlement ratio was 98.6%.

## 2. Maturity and survival benefits
### 2.1 Maturity payout
About 60 days before maturity, you will receive a discharge form. Submit it with a cancelled cheque and KYC documents; the maturity amount is credited on the maturity date if documents are received in time.

### 2.2 Survival benefits
For money-back plans, survival benefits are paid automatically to the registered bank account on the due date. Keep bank details updated to avoid delay.

## 3. Rejected or delayed claims
### 3.1 Grievance redressal
If you disagree with a claim decision, write to the Grievance Officer at grievance@surakshalife.example. We respond within 14 days. If unresolved, you can approach the Insurance Ombudsman or IRDAI's Bima Bharosa portal.
