# Policy Servicing Handbook
Suraksha Life Insurance — Customer Help Centre, document SRV-02 (v2.7)

## 1. Nominee
### 1.1 Who can be a nominee
Any person can be a nominee — usually a spouse, child, parent or sibling. If the nominee is a minor, you must also name an appointee (an adult) who will receive the money on the minor's behalf until they turn 18.

### 1.2 Changing the nominee
You can change the nominee any number of times during the policy term by submitting the Nomination Change form with the nominee's ID proof. There is no charge. A change of nominee cancels the previous nomination automatically. A nomination cannot be changed once the policy is assigned to a lender.

## 2. Assignment
### 2.1 What assignment means
Assignment transfers the rights of the policy to another person or institution (the assignee) — most often a bank, as security for a loan. Assignment can be absolute (all rights transfer permanently) or conditional (rights revert to you once the loan is repaid).

### 2.2 How to assign a policy
Submit the Assignment Request Form signed by both assignor (you) and assignee, along with proof of the assignment reason (e.g. the loan sanction letter). Processing takes 7 working days. Once assigned, the existing nomination is cancelled.

### 2.3 Reassignment after loan closure
When the loan is closed, the bank submits a reassignment letter, and you should register a fresh nomination since the earlier one was cancelled at assignment.

## 3. KYC and personal details
### 3.1 Updating address, mobile or email
Address can be updated with any valid address proof (Aadhaar, passport, driving licence, utility bill under 3 months old). Mobile number and email can be updated in the app after OTP verification on both the old and the new contact.

### 3.2 PAN, Aadhaar and FATCA
PAN is mandatory for annual premiums above ₹50,000. Every policyholder must also submit a FATCA/CRS self-declaration stating their country of tax residence, tax identification number (TIN — PAN for Indian residents), place of birth, nationality and father's name. Missing FATCA declarations can block payouts.

## 4. Loans and surrender
### 4.1 Policy loan
Policies with a surrender value can take a loan of up to 80% of the surrender value at 10% p.a. interest. Unpaid loan interest is deducted from future benefits.

### 4.2 Surrender
A policy acquires a surrender value after 2 full years of premiums. Surrender ends the policy permanently. Payout is credited within 7 working days of a complete request.
