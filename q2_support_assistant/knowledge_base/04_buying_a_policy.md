# Buying a Policy — Proposal & Onboarding Guide
Suraksha Life Insurance — Customer Help Centre, document ONB-04 (v1.9)

## 1. Proposal process
### 1.1 Documents to submit
A completed proposal form, photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN, passport or driving licence), address proof, recent photograph, income proof for sum assured above ₹50 lakh, and the first premium. A NACH mandate is recommended for renewals.

### 1.2 Declarations you will be asked to sign
Depending on the plan you may sign: a Benefit Illustration declaration (confirming you understood projected returns), a Suitability Profiler (confirming the plan matches your needs), a Moral Hazard questionnaire (for high sum assured relative to income), and a Multiple Policies consent form if you have bought more than one policy within six months.

### 1.3 Medical underwriting
Medical tests are required depending on age and sum assured — typically for applicants over 45 or sum assured over ₹1 crore. Tests are free and can be done at home.

## 2. After you buy
### 2.1 Free-look period
You have a free-look period of 30 days from receipt of the policy document to review it. If you cancel within this period, the premium is refunded after deducting stamp duty, medical costs and proportionate risk premium.

### 2.2 Tracking your application
Track your application status in the app with your application number. Typical issuance time is 3–5 working days after all documents and medical reports are received.

## 3. Tax benefits
### 3.1 Section 80C and 10(10D)
Premiums paid qualify for deduction under Section 80C (old tax regime) up to ₹1.5 lakh. Maturity proceeds are tax-free under Section 10(10D) if the annual premium is within 10% of sum assured and, for policies issued after 1 April 2023, total annual premiums across non-ULIP policies do not exceed ₹5 lakh.
